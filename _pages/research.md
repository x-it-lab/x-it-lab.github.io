# {{ page.title }}

Our research connects **Physical AI**, **trustworthy learning**, and **communication systems**. We study how AI can interact with the physical world and operate safely on real communication infrastructure.

<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Physical AI</h2>
<p class="research-desc">AI that interacts directly with the physical world through robots, drones, vehicles, and sensor networks. This is a core focus connecting the lab's work in AI and communications.</p>

</div>
</section>
<section class="research-card" id="federated-learning" style="scroll-margin-top: 6rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Federated &amp; Distributed Learning</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Learning across distributed devices and systems, considered in the context of real communication infrastructure and connected AI applications.</p>
<span class="chip chip-muted">Distributed intelligence</span>
</div>
</section>
<section class="research-card" id="trustworthy-ai" style="scroll-margin-top: 6rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Trustworthy AI</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Understanding how AI can operate safely in connected, real-world settings. This research area is part of Prof. Byung Chang Chung's focus in the lab.</p>
<span class="chip chip-muted">Trust &amp; safety</span>
</div>
</section>
<section class="research-card" id="network-security" style="scroll-margin-top: 6rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Network &amp; Communication Security</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Security in the networks and communication infrastructure used by connected systems, studied alongside trustworthy AI under Prof. Byung Chang Chung's direction.</p>
<span class="chip chip-muted">Communication security</span>
</div>
</section>
<section class="research-card" id="mimo-optimization" style="scroll-margin-top: 6rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">MIMO Optimization</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Optimization of multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) communication systems, a research focus led by Prof. Junbeom Kim.</p>
<span class="chip chip-muted">Communication optimization</span>
</div>
</section>
<section class="research-card" id="communication-systems" style="scroll-margin-top: 6rem;">
<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Communication Systems</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Communication-system technologies and optimization, connecting the lab's AI applications with the infrastructure on which they operate.</p>
<span class="chip chip-muted">Connected systems</span>
</div>
</section>
</div>
