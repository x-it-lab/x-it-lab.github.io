X-IT Lab works across IT, AI applications, and communication-system technologies. With **Physical AI** — AI that interacts directly with the real world through robots, drones, vehicles, and sensor networks — as a core focus, we study trustworthy AI, federated and distributed learning, and communication-system optimization to understand how AI can operate safely on real communication infrastructure. The X in our name stands for the **cross** of disciplines; our logo and motto carry that forward into real **connection** and **action**.

## Career Paths

Our graduates go on to work as AI/network engineers at telecom and network-equipment companies, AI security researchers at cybersecurity firms, Physical AI engineers at robotics and mobility companies, and pursue graduate study (MS/PhD) in related fields.
